## ELI5

The Agent board is a scoreboard. Today it has four different referees whispering who is playing (the kanban's rules, the status-tables rules, a patch on top, and another patch on top of that), and they disagree. So a ticket whose work just merged still looks "live", a ticket that is only waiting for its merge looks like it is "in review", and the board says "6 lanes" when the house rule is 4.

Since 2026-10-10 there is ONE referee that everyone agreed to use: the lane step log and its reader (`hooks/lane-state.mjs`, #3098 Leg A, merged). This plan makes the board, the status tables and the lane gates all read that one referee, deletes the whisperers, and proves it with frozen snapshots that reproduce each wrong row we saw (today's check 1 and the 2026-10-10 complaints): wrong before, right after.

Once the board is right, the clock starts: switch-off date = the day that fix is live + 7 days. The clock never restarts. Only a trust-breaking bug (board down, or a ticket shown in the wrong state) pauses it, and only until that bug is fixed. Then the paid Vercel kanban is paused (repo kept), the one launchd job that serves only Vercel is unloaded, and the hooks that mention "kanban" are audited (the ones that only keep local ticket files in sync stay). The launchd push job stays: it feeds the Agent board, not Vercel.

## Decisions

- D1 **One referee.** Lane membership, current step, slot-holding, waiting-to-merge and stale all come from `hooks/lane-state.mjs`. The board consumes it through the status-tables `--json` payload (so board == status tables by construction), and status-tables + `sole_lane_math` consume the reader (this executes #3098 Leg B under this ticket; #3098 keeps the kanban-selector half of Leg C, which the switch-off makes moot).
- D2 **Lanes = slot holders only.** The published `lanes` array holds exactly the reader's `holdsSlot=true` tickets (running work step, waiting-on-orchestrator, failed-back-to inside the between-step bound). `laneCap` stays the work ceiling, so "N of cap" is right by construction whatever the page computes.
- D3 **Ship-tail is its own list.** Tickets whose tail is OPEN (execution-review PASS with no ship-tail finish, or an open ship-tail/merge-wait step) go to a new `shipTail` array with the ship-tail ceiling; they are never in `lanes`.
- D4 **A merged leg is idle, not live.** A `waiting-to-merge` ticket whose newest ship-tail event/row is finished (non-error) is a merged leg: not in `lanes`, not in `shipTail`; its list row says so (`legMerged`), and it waits for the next leg. The reader must expose whether the tail is open (today it folds both into `waiting-to-merge`).
- D5 **Later-step evidence closes an earlier open start** (the #3109 shape, P5): an open `started` for step S with ANY newer evidence of a LATER step — a `started` or `finished` event, or a ledger row, open or closed — is treated as finished, never `stale`; the later step then decides the ticket's state under D3/D4. (Fold r1, F1: "open or closed" is load-bearing — an OPEN later ship-tail row means the tail is open, so the ticket is in `shipTail`; a CLOSED non-error later ship-tail row means a merged leg under D4.) This is a reader amendment (ai-brain) with its own fixture arms (9302 open tail, 9303 closed tail). (superseded by Amendment A2)
- D6 **List column follows the referee.** For an open ticket the board's list (In progress / Review) is derived from the reader state (running, waiting-on-orchestrator, failed-back-to → In progress; open tail → Review/merge; merged leg → In progress, labelled), not from `board.json.column` (export-board flips to in_review the moment any execution-review row lands — the 2026-10-10 "Needs-review lists an unmerged ticket").
- D7 **Fixtures, not live luck.** Every wrong row seen (check 1; 2026-10-10) is a frozen snapshot under `tests/fixtures/board-truth/` with `--now`, an `expected-old.json` (the actual wrong board answers, captured from issue #1 at 07:12Z with its `generatedAt`) and an `expected-new.json`. The multi-leg cadence (same-ticket respawn; next leg after a merged leg; merged leg behind an open earlier start) is a second, fully synthetic snapshot `tests/fixtures/board-truth/cadence/` so the frozen snapshot's slot arithmetic stays exactly what was measured (fold r1, F3/F4).
- D8 **The clock.** Fix ship date D = the first day the live issue #1 carries `laneSource: "lane-state"` AND a spot check (appended to the evidence file) scores zero wrong-state lane rows. `metadata.switch_off_date` = D + 7 (ISO date), written when D is known, never rewritten. A trust-breaking bug after D (board down; a ticket in the wrong state) pauses switch-off only until its fix ships, then switch off on the date, or at once if past. Cosmetic bugs get their own tickets and never delay.
- D9 **Switch-off keeps the Agent board's feed.** Unload only `com.user.kanban-board-watchdog`; keep `com.user.board-live-push` (feeds the Agent board) and `com.user.board-bookkeeping-sweep` (local store). Pause the Vercel project, keep the repo. The ticket text's step 3 ("unload board-live-push") is corrected by this plan.
- D10 Prefer data-only. The page strip for ship-tail / merged-leg is a separate cosmetic leg under the UI-task gate and never delays the clock.
